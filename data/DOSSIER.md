**Easy run HR cheat sheet (Managua hills):**
- Flat segments: stay 125–145 bpm. If you drift to 148–151 on a flat, slow down.
- Uphills: allow up to 158 bpm — hills will push HR regardless. Don't fight it.
- Downhills / post-climb recovery: bring HR back to 130–140 before the next effort.
- Check: if you can't hold a full conversation, you're too fast — slow down immediately.
- Practical test: if HR at the end of the first 10 minutes (flat section) is already >148, back off pace by 15–20 sec/km from the start.

### Recovery Protocol

**Recovery Triggers (Auto-Deload):**
- HRV ↓ > 20% below 7d baseline → drop to easy Z1 run or rest
- RHR ↑ ≥ 5 bpm above 7d baseline → drop intensity, no key workouts
- Feel ≥ 4 on 1–5 scale → rest or Z1 only
- Two+ triggers simultaneously → full rest day, reassess next morning

**Insomnia baseline — DO NOT use sleep hours as a standalone trigger:**
Ernesto has chronic insomnia. 5–5.5h sleep is his normal baseline, not a signal of degradation.
Sleep duration alone must never trigger deload. Only flag sleep as a concern when it coincides
with HRV ↓ >20% and/or RHR ↑ ≥5 bpm above baseline — combined signals only.

**Feel Scale:**
| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | Excellent (fully recovered) |
| 2 | Good (normal fatigue) |
| 3 | Moderate (manageable tiredness) |
| 4 | Fatigued (deload trigger) |
| 5 | Exhausted (complete rest required) |
